This course provides a comprehensive introduction to modern deep learning architectures and neural network fundamentals. Designed for 4th-year computer science undergraduates, students will explore foundational neural models, advanced optimization techniques, generative modeling, and attention-based architectures. Practical implementation and theoretical understanding are combined over an intensive 8-week curriculum. Prerequisites: Linear algebra, basic probability, and Python programming.

Lecture

WeekModule TopicDescription
Week 1 (09/14/2026)Foundations of Deep Learning (slides)Introduction to AI, Deep Learning, and Course Logistics.
Week 2 (09/21/2026)Artificial Neural Networks (Slides)Introduction to neural networks, perceptrons, activation functions, and feedforward dynamics.
Week 3 (09/28/2026)Optimization and Training (Slides)Gradient descent, backpropagation algorithms, momentum, Adam optimizer, and learning rate scheduling. Overfitting prevention, dropout, weight decay (L1/L2), batch normalization, and data augmentation..
Week 4 (10/12/2026)Convolutional Neural Networks (Slides)Spatial convolutions, pooling layers, feature maps, and modern CNN architectures (ResNet, VGG).
Week 5 (10/19/2026)RNN & LSTM NetworksSequential data modeling, recurrent neural networks, vanishing gradients, and Long Short-Term Memory units.
Week 6 (10/26/2026)Transformers and AttentionSelf-attention mechanisms, multi-head attention, positional encoding, and transformer architectures.
Week 7 (11/02/2026)Generative ModelsAutoencoders, Variational Autoencoders (VAEs), Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), and diffusion models.
Week 8 (11/09/2026)Advanced TopicsReinforcement learning integration, model deployment, efficient inference, and state-of-the-art frontiers.
